# Roadmap

## Done

1. **Prototype.** Waves, the crossbow, hearts and the on-screen keyboard with finger colors.
2. **Learning engine.** Five modes, letters growing into words, adaptive pace, weak-key practice, profiles, the daily 20-minute hunt and the Capitals toggle.
3. **Rewards.**
   - Coins and a shop with hunters, outfits and bolt skins.
   - Achievement unlocks and the ultimate Sunfire Champion outfit.
   - Seven settings with matching battle music and fades between waves.
4. **No peeking.** The on-screen keyboard is covered in the real game and only shown in a practice mode that earns nothing.
5. **Skips mastered warm-ups.** Each hunter's hunts start at the all-keys wave once they've cleared it cleanly twice.
6. **Variety and careful aim.**
   - New enemies and a boss for each setting, each with its own trick.
   - Wrong keys cost points, jam the crossbow when they come in a row, and bring the Mega Mutant when there are too many in a wave.

7. **Math Hunt, phase 1.** Times tables on the enemies, typed answers that fire on a match, a fact engine with five levels and spaced review, a scouting round, Smart Hunt, three places with their bosses, new-fact cards and a daily math goal.
8. **Math Hunt, phases 2 and 3.**
   - A grown-ups page behind a PIN: the fact grid, facts to watch, answer times by week, a practice log, the typing heat map, settings and a copyable report.
   - All nine places on a map, from ×1 to the castle, with mastery stamps and two new bosses (the Beetle Baron and Countess Weaver), plus Count Moonfang's return.
   - A math job for every enemy, hint pictures, Mixed Review, math practice, and the Mega Mutant carrying missed facts.

## Next

### Math Hunt
- **New music from Suno.** A math menu theme, battle loops for the Spider Tower and the Castle, and a short Table Mastered fanfare. For now those places reuse the Bat Cave and Courtyard tracks, and the stamp plays the built-in fanfare.
- **Reordering the places** from the grown-ups page, to match what the class is doing at school.
- **Phase 4: beyond the tables.** Division by fact family, adding and subtracting, the 11s and 12s, giant bats that split a hard fact into two easier ones, and math rewards (The Professor hunter, a number bolt and the Times Table Champion outfit).

### Ship and test
- Publish on GitHub Pages.
- Play-test on the iPad Pro with its hardware keyboard: input, audio unlock, sizing and how long saves last.
- Watch the kids play a few sessions and tune speed, word lists and coin prices.

### More bosses
- A boss gallery in the shop that shows who each kid has beaten.
- A last-stand phrase typed in one go at the end of a boss fight.

### Advanced mode (unlocks with Sunfire Champion)
- Full sentences with real capitalization and punctuation: periods, commas, question marks and apostrophes.
- One vampire per sentence, or a sentence split across a line of vampires that must be typed in order.
- Its own WPM and accuracy goals, plus new rewards: a cosmetic aura, a golden crossbow and a title on the profile card.
- Possibly a "story" sentence pack written for 8 to 11 year olds, and a pack of fun facts.

### Other ideas
- Optional saving across devices.
- More hunters and outfits, plus seasonal ones.
