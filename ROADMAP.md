# Roadmap

## Done

1. **Prototype.** Waves, the crossbow, hearts and the on-screen keyboard with finger colors.
2. **Learning engine.** Five modes, letters growing into words, adaptive pace, weak-key practice, profiles, the daily 20-minute hunt and the Capitals toggle.
3. **Rewards.**
   - Coins and a shop with hunters, outfits and bolt skins.
   - Achievement unlocks and the ultimate Sunfire Champion outfit.
   - Seven settings with matching battle music and fades between waves.
4. **No peeking.** The on-screen keyboard is covered in the real game and only shown in a practice mode that earns nothing.
5. **Skips mastered warm-ups.** Each hunter's hunts start at the all-keys wave once they've cleared it cleanly twice.

## Next

### Ship and test
- Publish on GitHub Pages.
- Play-test on the iPad Pro with its hardware keyboard: input, audio unlock, sizing and how long saves last.
- Watch the kids play a few sessions and tune speed, word lists and coin prices.

### More bosses
Count Moonfang is the only boss today. Ideas:
- **Each setting gets its own boss**, chosen to match the scene:
  - A giant bat queen for the bat cave
  - A werewolf for the forest
  - A mummy for the crypt
  - A ghost knight for the courtyard
- **Boss tricks that teach skills:**
  - Swapping between shielded and open words
  - Calling in small bats that carry single letters
  - A last-stand phrase typed in one go
- **A boss gallery** in the shop that shows who each kid has beaten.

### Advanced mode (unlocks with Sunfire Champion)
- Full sentences with real capitalization and punctuation: periods, commas, question marks and apostrophes.
- One vampire per sentence, or a sentence split across a line of vampires that must be typed in order.
- Its own WPM and accuracy goals, plus new rewards: a cosmetic aura, a golden crossbow and a title on the profile card.
- Possibly a "story" sentence pack written for 8 to 11 year olds, and a pack of fun facts.

### Other ideas
- A progress page for parents with key accuracy heat maps and minutes practiced per day.
- Optional saving across devices.
- More hunters and outfits, plus seasonal ones.
